What is MusicBoxd?

MusicBoxd is a place for music fans to keep track of the albums and songs they listen to. You can rate albums, write reviews, create lists and discover new music.

Discover music

Explore popular music, discover recommended albums and search for artists, albums and songs. MusicBoxd brings different music services together to make discovering something new easier.

Share your taste

Rate and review the music you listen to, create your own lists and connect with friends to see what they are listening to and enjoying.

Built for music fans

MusicBoxd was created as a social space for people who want to explore music, share their opinions and keep a record of their listening journey.
